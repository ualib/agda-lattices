### The generators of a free lattice

This is the [Lattices.Free.Prime][] module of the [agda-lattices][] library.

[Lattices.Prime][] proves Lemmas 1.3 and 1.4 and Corollary 1.6 of [Freese,
Ježek, and Nation (1995)][] for any lattice generated by a set satisfying
condition (†).  This module reads them in the free lattice `FL(X)`, for any
type `X` of generators, as follows:

+  **Lemma 1.2** in `FL(X)`: the generators satisfy (†)
   (`FL-satisfies†`{.AgdaFunction}).  The book maps `FL(X)` onto the
   two-element lattice, sending one of the two disjoint sets to `1`, which
   decides membership in a set of generators; here Whitman's condition along
   two lists (`W-list`{.AgdaFunction} of [Lattices.Free.Canonical.Basic][])
   and the primality of the generators (rules 4 and 5) find the shared
   generator, with no decision.  `FL(X)` is generated by `X`
   (`FL-onto`{.AgdaFunction}).
+  **Corollary 1.5** in `FL(X)`: the meet of a finite nonempty set of
   generators is join prime and its join is meet prime
   (`FL-prime-meets-joins`{.AgdaFunction}).  Its other two clauses are in the
   library already: every generator is join prime and meet prime
   (`FL-gen-prime`{.AgdaFunction} and `FL-gen-primeᵈ`{.AgdaFunction} of
   [Lattices.Bounded.Covers][]), and two comparable generators are equal
   (`ℊ≤ℊ-inv`{.AgdaFunction} of [Lattices.Free.Whitman][]).
+  **Lemma 1.4** in `FL(X)`: its join prime elements are the meets of finite
   nonempty sets of generators, its meet prime elements the joins
   (`FL-joinPrime→meet`{.AgdaFunction}, `FL-meetPrime→join`{.AgdaFunction}),
   so its generators are exactly its elements both join prime and meet prime
   (`FL-doublyPrime⇔generator`{.AgdaFunction}), and a partition of the generators
   partitions `FL(X)` into a filter and an ideal (`FL-partition`{.AgdaFunction}).
+  **Corollary 1.6** in `FL(X)`: every generating family contains the
   generators (`FL-generators-in-family`{.AgdaFunction}; the stronger form for
   any family, `generator-in-image`{.AgdaFunction}, is in
   [Lattices.Free.Generation][]), and every automorphism is induced by a
   permutation of `X` (`FL-automorphism-permutation`{.AgdaFunction}).
   Conversely every permutation of `X` induces an automorphism
   (`permutation-automorphism`{.AgdaFunction}), and the two passages are
   mutually inverse (`permutation-of-automorphism`{.AgdaFunction},
   `automorphism-of-permutation`{.AgdaFunction}) and respect composition
   (`permutation-automorphism-∘`{.AgdaFunction}): the automorphism group of
   `FL(X)` is the symmetric group on `X`, the book's last sentence of the
   corollary.

#### Lemma 1.2: the generators satisfy (†)

The meet and join of `FL X`{.AgdaFunction} are the formal ones by definition, so
the meet and join of a list in `FL X`{.AgdaFunction} are the term-level ones
of [Lattices.Free.Canonical.Basic][] (`FL-⋀⁺`{.AgdaFunction},
`FL-⋁⁺`{.AgdaFunction}), by induction on the list.  If the meet of the
generators in `s ∷ ss` lies below the join of those in `t ∷ ts`, Whitman's
condition along the two lists (`W-list`{.AgdaFunction}) puts a meetand `ℊ x`
below the join, or the meet below a joinand `ℊ y`; in the first case `ℊ x` is
below a joinand by rule 4, in the second a meetand is below `ℊ y` by rule 5,
and two comparable generators are equal by rule 1.  So a generator occurs in
both lists (`FL-satisfies†`{.AgdaFunction}), which is Lemma 1.2 for `FL(X)`.
Every element of `FL(X)` is the value of itself (`FL-onto`{.AgdaFunction}), so
`X` generates `FL(X)`.

```agda
module _ {X : Type χ} where
  open Setoid 𝔻[ FL X .proj₁ ]   using ( reflexive )

  -- The meet of a list in FL X is its term-level meet.
  FL-⋀⁺ : ∀ b bs → ⋀⁺ (FL X) b bs ≡ ⋀̇⁺ b bs
  FL-⋀⁺ b []        = refl
  FL-⋀⁺ b (c ∷ cs)  = cong (b ∧̇_) (FL-⋀⁺ c cs)

  -- The join of a list in FL X is its term-level join.
  FL-⋁⁺ : ∀ b bs → ⋁⁺ (FL X) b bs ≡ ⋁̇⁺ b bs
  FL-⋁⁺ b []        = refl
  FL-⋁⁺ b (c ∷ cs)  = cong (b ∨̇_) (FL-⋁⁺ c cs)

  -- Lemma 1.2: in FL(X), a meet of generators lies below a join of generators
  -- only when they share one.
  FL-satisfies† : Satisfies† (FL X) ℊ
  FL-satisfies† s ss t ts p =
    shared (W-list  (map ℊ ss) (map ℊ ts) ( subst₂ _≤ʷ_  (FL-⋀⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss))
                                                         (FL-⋁⁺ (ℊ t) (map ℊ ts))
                                                         (≤→≤ʷ p)) )
    where
    -- A meetand below the join, or the meet below a joinand, names a shared generator.
    shared :  Any.Any (_≤ʷ ⋁̇⁺ (ℊ t) (map ℊ ts)) (ℊ s ∷ map ℊ ss) ⊎
              Any.Any (⋀̇⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss) ≤ʷ_) (ℊ t ∷ map ℊ ts)
              → Σ[ x ∈ X ] (x ∈ s ∷ ss × x ∈ t ∷ ts)
    shared (inj₁ meetand) =
      let (x , x∈S , x≤) = find (Any.map⁻ meetand)
      in  x , x∈S , Any.map ℊ≤ℊ-inv (Any.map⁻ (gen-⋁⁺-prime x≤))
    shared (inj₂ joinand) =
      let (y , y∈T , ≤y) = find (Any.map⁻ joinand)
      in  y , Any.map (λ e → sym (ℊ≤ℊ-inv e)) (Any.map⁻ (⋀⁺-gen-below ≤y)) , y∈T

  -- X generates FL(X): every element is the value of itself.
  FL-onto : IsSurjective (FL-extend (FL X) ℊ)
  FL-onto {b} = eq b (reflexive (sym (⟦⟧-ℊ b)))
```

#### Corollary 1.5 and Lemma 1.4 in `FL(X)`

The general statements of [Lattices.Prime][] at `𝑳 = FL X`{.AgdaFunction} and
`η = ℊ`, with the meets and joins of lists read as terms.

```agda
  -- Corollary 1.5: in FL(X), meets of generators are join prime and joins of
  -- generators meet prime.
  FL-prime-meets-joins : ∀ s ss
    →  IsJoinPrime (FL X) (⋀̇⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)) ×
       IsJoinPrime (dualLattice (FL X)) (⋁̇⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss))
  FL-prime-meets-joins s ss =
    let (jp , mp) = prime-meets-joins (FL X) ℊ FL-onto FL-satisfies† s ss
    in  subst (IsJoinPrime (FL X)) (FL-⋀⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)) jp ,
        subst (IsJoinPrime (dualLattice (FL X))) (FL-⋁⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)) mp

  -- Lemma 1.4 (1) in FL(X): a join prime element is a meet of finitely many generators.
  FL-joinPrime→meet : ∀ {w} → IsJoinPrime (FL X) w
    → Σ[ s ∈ X ] Σ[ ss ∈ List X ] w ≈ʷ ⋀̇⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)
  FL-joinPrime→meet jp = let (s , ss , e) = joinPrime→meet (FL X) ℊ FL-onto jp in
    s , ss , subst (_ ≈ʷ_) (FL-⋀⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)) e

  -- Lemma 1.4 (2) in FL(X): a meet prime element is a join of finitely many generators.
  FL-meetPrime→join : ∀ {w} → IsJoinPrime (dualLattice (FL X)) w
    → Σ[ s ∈ X ] Σ[ ss ∈ List X ] w ≈ʷ ⋁̇⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)
  FL-meetPrime→join mp = let (s , ss , e) = meetPrime→join (FL X) ℊ FL-onto mp in
    s , ss , subst (_ ≈ʷ_) (FL-⋁⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)) e

  -- In FL(X), the generators are exactly the elements both join prime and meet prime.
  FL-doublyPrime⇔generator : ∀ w
    →  (IsJoinPrime (FL X) w × IsJoinPrime (dualLattice (FL X)) w) ⇔
       (Σ[ x ∈ X ] w ≈ʷ ℊ x)
  FL-doublyPrime⇔generator = doublyPrime⇔generator (FL X) ℊ FL-onto FL-satisfies†

  -- Lemma 1.4 (4) in FL(X): a partition of X partitions FL(X) into a filter and an ideal.
  FL-partition : (Y : Pred X ℓ₁) (Z : Pred X ℓ₂)
    → (∀ x → Y x ⊎ Z x)
    → (∀ {x} → Y x → Z x → ⊥)
    → ∀ w
    →  (GeneratedFilter (FL X) ℊ Y w ⊎ GeneratedIdeal (FL X) ℊ Z w) ×
       (GeneratedFilter (FL X) ℊ Y w → GeneratedIdeal (FL X) ℊ Z w → ⊥)
  FL-partition Y Z cover apart = partition (FL X) ℊ Y Z cover FL-onto FL-satisfies† apart
```

#### Corollary 1.6 in `FL(X)`

Every family generating `FL(X)` contains the generators
(`FL-generators-in-family`{.AgdaFunction}), and every automorphism `φ` of
`FL(X)` permutes them (`FL-automorphism-permutation`{.AgdaFunction}), with
`φ(t)` the substitution of the permutation in `t`
(`FL-automorphism-induced`{.AgdaFunction}): a homomorphism of free lattices is
the substitution of its values at the generators (`hom-induced`{.AgdaFunction}
of [Lattices.Free.Substitution][]).  This is `automorphism-induced`{.AgdaFunction}
of [Lattices.Prime][] read in `FL(X)`; deriving it from that statement, through
the evaluation of terms in `FL(X)`, cost 7 s of conversion checking, and this
proof costs nothing measurable.

```agda
  open Inverse

  -- Corollary 1.6 (1) in FL(X): every generating family contains the generators.
  FL-generators-in-family : {J : Type ι} (κ : J → LatTerm X)
    → IsSurjective (FL-extend (FL X) κ) → ∀ x → Σ[ j ∈ J ] ℊ x ≈ʷ κ j
  FL-generators-in-family = generators-in-family (FL X) ℊ FL-onto FL-satisfies†

  -- Corollary 1.6 (2) in FL(X): every automorphism is induced by a permutation of X.
  FL-automorphism-permutation : (φ : FL X .proj₁ ≅ FL X .proj₁)
    → Σ[ π ∈ X ↔ X ] (∀ x → _≅_.to φ .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ ℊ x ≈ʷ ℊ (to π x))
  FL-automorphism-permutation = automorphism-permutation (FL X) ℊ FL-onto FL-satisfies†

  -- An automorphism of FL(X) is the substitution of its permutation.
  FL-automorphism-induced : (φ : FL X .proj₁ ≅ FL X .proj₁) (t : LatTerm X)
    → _≅_.to φ .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ t ≈ʷ t [ ℊ ∘ to (FL-automorphism-permutation φ .proj₁) ]
  FL-automorphism-induced φ t =
    ≈ʷ-trans  (hom-induced  (_≅_.to φ) t)
                            ([]-congʳ t (FL-automorphism-permutation φ .proj₂))
    where
    π : X ↔ X
    π = FL-automorphism-permutation φ .proj₁
```

#### The automorphism group is the symmetric group

A permutation `π` of `X` induces the automorphism of `FL(X)` that substitutes
`π` for the generators (`permutation-automorphism`{.AgdaFunction}), with the
substitution of `π⁻¹` as its inverse: substituting one and then the other is
substituting the composite (`[]-⨟`{.AgdaFunction}), which fixes every
generator.  The permutation of this automorphism is `π`
(`permutation-of-automorphism`{.AgdaFunction}), since the automorphism sends
`ℊ x` to `ℊ (π x)` and generators are equal only when their indices are.  The
automorphism of the permutation of `φ` agrees with `φ`
(`automorphism-of-permutation`{.AgdaFunction}), by
`FL-automorphism-induced`{.AgdaFunction}.  And the automorphism of a composite
permutation is the composite of the automorphisms
(`permutation-automorphism-∘`{.AgdaFunction}), by `[]-⨟`{.AgdaFunction}.  So
`φ ↦ π` is a bijection of the automorphisms of `FL(X)`, up to the equality of
`FL(X)`, onto the permutations of `X`, which respects composition.

```agda
  private
    -- Substituting two maps of generators that compose to the identity fixes a term.
    inverse-[] : (f g : X → X) → (∀ x → f (g x) ≡ x) → ∀ t → t [ ℊ ∘ g ] [ ℊ ∘ f ] ≈ʷ t
    inverse-[] f g fg t =
      subst (_≈ʷ t) ([]-⨟ t (ℊ ∘ g) (ℊ ∘ f))
            (≈ʷ-trans ([]-congʳ t {σ = (ℊ ∘ g) ⨟ (ℊ ∘ f)} {τ = ℊ} (λ x → reflexive (cong ℊ (fg x))))
                      (reflexive ([]-ℊ t)))

  -- A permutation of X induces an automorphism of FL(X).
  permutation-automorphism : X ↔ X → FL X .proj₁ ≅ FL X .proj₁
  permutation-automorphism π =
    mkiso (subst-hom (ℊ ∘ to π)) (subst-hom (ℊ ∘ from π))
          (inverse-[] (to π) (from π) (strictlyInverseˡ π))
          (inverse-[] (from π) (to π) (strictlyInverseʳ π))

  -- The permutation of the automorphism of π is π.
  permutation-of-automorphism : (π : X ↔ X) (x : X)
    → to (FL-automorphism-permutation (permutation-automorphism π) .proj₁) x ≡ to π x
  permutation-of-automorphism π x =
    sym (ℊ-injective (FL-automorphism-permutation (permutation-automorphism π) .proj₂ x))

  -- The automorphism of the permutation of φ agrees with φ.
  automorphism-of-permutation : (φ : FL X .proj₁ ≅ FL X .proj₁) (t : LatTerm X)
    → _≅_.to (permutation-automorphism (FL-automorphism-permutation φ .proj₁)) .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ t
      ≈ʷ _≅_.to φ .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ t
  automorphism-of-permutation = ≈ʷ-sym ∘₂ FL-automorphism-induced

  -- The automorphism of a composite permutation is the composite of the automorphisms.
  permutation-automorphism-∘ : (π σ : X ↔ X) (t : LatTerm X)
    → _≅_.to (permutation-automorphism (π ↔-∘ σ)) .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ t
      ≡ _≅_.to (permutation-automorphism π) .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ (_≅_.to (permutation-automorphism σ) .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ t)
  permutation-automorphism-∘ π σ t = []-⨟ t (ℊ ∘ to σ) (ℊ ∘ to π)
```

#### The statements of Lemma 1.2 and Corollaries 1.5 and 1.6

Each of the three results is one definition whose type is the statement, with
its proof beside it, built from the names above; [Lattices.Free.FJN][] lists
them with the rest of Chapter I.  A statement quantifies over the levels of
the generators and the lattices it speaks of, so it is a type in `Setω`, and a
statement in several parts is a record with one field per part, in the order
the book gives them.  The order of `FL(X)` is `_≤ʷ_`{.AgdaDatatype} and its
equality `_≈ʷ_`{.AgdaFunction}, by Whitman's theorem
(`WhitmansTheorem`{.AgdaFunction} of [Lattices.Free.Universal][]).

`FJN-1·2`{.AgdaFunction} is Lemma 1.2: the generators of `FL(X)` satisfy (†),
`⋀S ≤ ⋁T` implies that `S` and `T` share a generator, for finite nonempty
`S, T ⊆ X`, given as lists.  The book states it for the relatively free
lattice of any nontrivial variety; the library has `FL(X)` alone.  The sets
are nonempty because a meet or join of lattice terms is; read with the empty
meet as a top, (†) would fail in `FL(X)` for finite nonempty `X`.

`FJN-1·5`{.AgdaFunction} is Corollary 1.5 in `FL(X)`, again not in every
relatively free lattice: (1) for a finite nonempty `S ⊆ X`, `⋀S` is join prime
and `⋁S` is meet prime; (2) every generator is join prime and meet prime; (3)
`x ≤ y` for generators implies `x = y`.  Meet primality is join primality in
the dual lattice, as in [Lattices.Bounded.Covers][].

`FJN-1·6`{.AgdaFunction} is Corollary 1.6.  Its parts (1) and (2) are for any
lattice `L` generated by `X` (`FL-extend`{.AgdaFunction} onto) with (†): (1)
every generating set of `L`, given as a family `κ`, contains `X`, up to the
equality of `L`; (2) every automorphism of `L` is induced by a permutation of
`X`.  Its last sentence, part (3), is stated for `FL(X)` and not for every
relatively free lattice: the automorphism group of `FL(X)` is isomorphic to the
symmetric group on `X`, by the map `P` that sends a permutation to the
substitution it induces.  `P π` is `π` on the generators, `P` sends a composite
to the composite, every automorphism is some `P π`, and `P π` and `P σ` agree
only when `π` and `σ` do; equality of automorphisms is agreement at every
element, in the equality of `FL(X)`.

```agda
-- FJN Lemma 1.2, in FL(X): the generators satisfy condition (†).
FJN-1·2 : Setω
FJN-1·2 = ∀ {χ} {X : Type χ} → Satisfies† (FL X) ℊ

fjn-1·2 : FJN-1·2
fjn-1·2 = FL-satisfies†

-- FJN Corollary 1.5, in FL(X): meets and joins of generators are prime, and
-- comparable generators are equal.
record FJN-1·5 : Setω where
  field
    part-1 : ∀ {χ} {X : Type χ} (s : X) (ss : List X)
             →  IsJoinPrime (FL X) (⋀̇⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss)) ×
                IsJoinPrime (dualLattice (FL X)) (⋁̇⁺ (ℊ s) (map ℊ ss))
    part-2 : ∀ {χ} {X : Type χ} (x : X)
             → IsJoinPrime (FL X) (ℊ x) × IsJoinPrime (dualLattice (FL X)) (ℊ x)
    part-3 : ∀ {χ} {X : Type χ} {x y : X} → ℊ x ≤ʷ ℊ y → x ≡ y

fjn-1·5 : FJN-1·5
fjn-1·5 = record  { part-1 = FL-prime-meets-joins
                  ; part-2 = λ x → FL-prime-meets-joins x []
                  ; part-3 = ℊ≤ℊ-inv
                  }

-- FJN Corollary 1.6: generating sets contain the generators, automorphisms
-- permute them, and the automorphism group of FL(X) is the symmetric group.
record FJN-1·6 : Setω where
  field
    part-1 : ∀ {α ρ χ ι} (𝓛 : Lattice α ρ) {X : Type χ} (η : X → 𝕌[ 𝓛 .proj₁ ])
             → IsSurjective (FL-extend 𝓛 η) → Satisfies† 𝓛 η
             → {J : Type ι} (κ : J → 𝕌[ 𝓛 .proj₁ ]) → IsSurjective (FL-extend 𝓛 κ)
             → let open Setoid 𝔻[ 𝓛 .proj₁ ] using ( _≈_ ) in
               ∀ x → Σ[ j ∈ J ] η x ≈ κ j
    part-2 : ∀ {α ρ χ} (𝓛 : Lattice α ρ) {X : Type χ} (η : X → 𝕌[ 𝓛 .proj₁ ])
             → IsSurjective (FL-extend 𝓛 η) → Satisfies† 𝓛 η
             → let open Setoid 𝔻[ 𝓛 .proj₁ ] using ( _≈_ ) in
               (φ : 𝓛 .proj₁ ≅ 𝓛 .proj₁)
             → Σ[ π ∈ X ↔ X ] (∀ x → _≅_.to φ .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ η x ≈ η (Inverse.to π x))
    part-3 : ∀ {χ} {X : Type χ}
             → let ap : FL X .proj₁ ≅ FL X .proj₁ → LatTerm X → LatTerm X
                   ap φ t = _≅_.to φ .proj₁ ⟨$⟩ t
               in
               Σ[ P ∈ (X ↔ X → FL X .proj₁ ≅ FL X .proj₁) ]
                  (∀ π x → ap (P π) (ℊ x) ≈ʷ ℊ (Inverse.to π x))
                × (∀ π σ t → ap (P (π ↔-∘ σ)) t ≈ʷ ap (P π) (ap (P σ) t))
                × (∀ φ → Σ[ π ∈ X ↔ X ] (∀ t → ap (P π) t ≈ʷ ap φ t))
                × (∀ π σ → (∀ t → ap (P π) t ≈ʷ ap (P σ) t)
                   → ∀ x → Inverse.to π x ≡ Inverse.to σ x)

fjn-1·6 : FJN-1·6
fjn-1·6 = record
  { part-1 = λ 𝓛 η onto dag κ → generators-in-family 𝓛 η onto dag κ
  ; part-2 = automorphism-permutation
  ; part-3 =  permutation-automorphism
             , (λ π x → ≈ʷ-refl)
             , (λ π σ t → Setoid.reflexive 𝔻[ FL _ .proj₁ ] (permutation-automorphism-∘ π σ t))
             , (λ φ → FL-automorphism-permutation φ .proj₁ , automorphism-of-permutation φ)
             , (λ π σ e x → ℊ-injective (e (ℊ x)))
  }
```
