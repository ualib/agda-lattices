### Membership in a finitely generated sublattice of a free lattice

This is the [Lattices.Free.Membership][] module of the [agda-lattices][]
library.

A finitely generated sublattice of a free lattice `FL(Y)` is the image of a
homomorphism `σ : FL(n) → FL(Y)`, which is substitution by
`σ : Sub (Fin n) Y` ([Lattices.Free.Substitution][]).  Whether an element `w`
of `FL(Y)` lies in it is decidable, when the equality of `Y` is
(`inImage?`{.AgdaFunction}).  The decision is the least preimage: the
homomorphism is lower bounded, so the elements `a` of `FL(n)` whose images lie
above `w` have a least one `β(w)` when there are any, and then `w` lies in the
image exactly when `β(w)` is sent to `w` (`inImage⇔β`{.AgdaFunction}): if
`a [ σ ] = w`, then `β(w) ≤ a`, so `w ≤ β(w) [ σ ] ≤ a [ σ ] = w`.  And there are
elements above `w` exactly when `w` lies below the image of the top of
`FL(n)` (`leastAbove⇔below-top`{.AgdaFunction}), for `n ≥ 1`, where `FL(n)` has
a top ([Lattices.Free.Extrema][]).  Together, `w` lies in the image exactly
when `w ≤ σ(x₁ ∨ ⋯ ∨ xₙ)` and `β(w) [ σ ] = w` (`inImage⇔`{.AgdaFunction}).
The image is all of `FL(Y)` exactly when every generator is one of the images
`σ i` (`onto⇔generators-are-images`{.AgdaFunction}, by Corollary 1.6), and
for finitely many generators it is not all of `FL(Y)` exactly when some
generator is none of them (`not-onto⇔missed-generator`{.AgdaFunction}).

[Freese, Ježek, and Nation (1995)][] obtain the lower bound `β` of a
homomorphism from a finitely generated lattice to `FL(Y)` from Theorem 2.6 and
Lemma 2.7, with `D(FL(Y)) = FL(Y)` (Corollary 2.11), and compute it by
Corollary 2.8, which defines `β(w)` exactly for `w ≤ σ(1)`.  This module takes
it from Kostinsky's Lemma 3.1 instead, `leastAbove?`{.AgdaFunction} of
[Lattices.Free.Bounded][], which computes `β(w)` by recursion on `w`, or
reports that no image lies above `w`.

#### The image of a substitution

`InImage w`{.AgdaFunction} says that `w` is the image of an element of
`FL(n)`.  With the equality of `Y` decided, `leastAbove?`{.AgdaFunction}
returns the least preimage above `w`, or that there is none
(`NoneAbove`{.AgdaFunction}).  If `w` is an image, the least preimage `β`
exists and its image lies below that of every preimage above `w`
(`least-image`{.AgdaFunction}), hence below `w`, and above `w` by definition:
`β` is sent to `w` (`inImage⇔β`{.AgdaFunction}).  The decision
(`inImage?`{.AgdaFunction}) asks `leastAbove?`{.AgdaFunction} for `β` and then
decides `β [ σ ] ≤ʷ w` by Whitman's procedure.

```agda
module _ {n : ℕ} {Y : Type υ} (σ : Sub (Fin n) Y) where
  -- w lies in the sublattice generated by σ, the image of FL(n) under σ.
  InImage : LatTerm Y → Type υ
  InImage w = Σ[ a ∈ LatTerm (Fin n) ] a [ σ ] ≈ʷ w

  -- The image of the least preimage above w lies below every image above w.
  least-image : ∀ {w} (β : LeastAbove σ w) → ∀ a → w ≤ʷ a [ σ ] → LeastAbove.elt β [ σ ] ≤ʷ a [ σ ]
  least-image β a p = []-mono σ (LeastAbove.least β a p)

module _ {n : ℕ} {Y : Type υ} (_≟ʸ_ : DecidableEquality Y) (σ : Sub (Fin n) Y) where
  open Decision _≟ʸ_ using ( _≤ʷ?_ )

  private
    -- The least preimage above w, when some image lies above w.
    least-from : ∀ {w} a → w ≤ʷ a [ σ ] → LeastAbove σ w ⊎ NoneAbove σ w → LeastAbove σ w
    least-from a p (inj₁ β)     = β
    least-from a p (inj₂ none)  = ⊥-elim (none a p)

  -- The least preimage above w, given an image above w.
  β-above : ∀ {w} a → w ≤ʷ a [ σ ] → LeastAbove σ w
  β-above {w} a p = least-from a p (leastAbove? _≟ʸ_ σ w)

  -- w lies in the image exactly when the least preimage above it exists and is sent to w.
  inImage⇔β : ∀ {w} → InImage σ w ⇔ (Σ[ β ∈ LeastAbove σ w ] LeastAbove.elt β [ σ ] ≈ʷ w)
  inImage⇔β {w} = mk⇔ to from
    where
    to : InImage σ w → Σ[ β ∈ LeastAbove σ w ] LeastAbove.elt β [ σ ] ≈ʷ w
    to (a , e₁ , e₂) = β-above a e₂  , ≤ʷ-trans (least-image σ (β-above a e₂) a e₂) e₁
                                     , LeastAbove.above (β-above a e₂)

    from : Σ[ β ∈ LeastAbove σ w ] LeastAbove.elt β [ σ ] ≈ʷ w → InImage σ w
    from (β , e) = LeastAbove.elt β , e

  private
    -- Decide membership from the answer of leastAbove?.
    decide : ∀ {w} → LeastAbove σ w ⊎ NoneAbove σ w → Dec (InImage σ w)
    decide (inj₂ none)  = no λ (a , _ , e) → none a e
    decide {w} (inj₁ β) =
      map′  (λ p → LeastAbove.elt β , p , LeastAbove.above β)
            (λ (a , e₁ , e₂) → ≤ʷ-trans (least-image σ β a e₂) e₁)
            (LeastAbove.elt β [ σ ] ≤ʷ? w)

  -- Membership in a finitely generated sublattice of FL(Y) is decidable.
  inImage? : ∀ w → Dec (InImage σ w)
  inImage? w = decide (leastAbove? _≟ʸ_ σ w)
```

#### Corollary 2.8: where the least preimage exists

Let `⊤` be a top element of `FL(n)`, which exists exactly when `n ≥ 1`
([Lattices.Free.Extrema][]): the join `x₁ ∨ ⋯ ∨ xₙ` of the generators, or
anything equal to it.  A least preimage above `w` exists exactly when `w` lies
below `⊤ [ σ ]` (`leastAbove⇔below-top`{.AgdaFunction}): every preimage lies
below `⊤`, so its image below `⊤ [ σ ]`; and `⊤` is itself a preimage above
such a `w`.  So `w` lies in the image exactly when `w ≤ ⊤ [ σ ]` and the least
preimage it then has is sent to `w` (`inImage⇔`{.AgdaFunction}), the form of
the criterion with the two conditions apart.

```agda
  module _ ((⊤₁ , ⊤₂) : TopOf (FL (Fin n))) where
    -- The image of the top.
    image-of-top : LatTerm Y
    image-of-top = ⊤₁ [ σ ]

    -- A least preimage above w exists exactly when w lies below the image of the top.
    leastAbove⇔below-top : ∀ {w} → LeastAbove σ w ⇔ w ≤ʷ image-of-top
    leastAbove⇔below-top {w} = mk⇔ to (β-above ⊤₁)
      where
      to : LeastAbove σ w → w ≤ʷ image-of-top
      to β = ≤ʷ-trans (LeastAbove.above β) ([]-mono σ (≤→≤ʷ (⊤₂  (LeastAbove.elt β))))

    -- w is in the image exactly when it is below the image of the top and its
    -- least preimage is sent to it.
    inImage⇔ : ∀ {w}
      →  InImage σ w
          ⇔ (Σ[ p ∈ w ≤ʷ image-of-top ] LeastAbove.elt (β-above ⊤₁ p) [ σ ] ≈ʷ w)

    inImage⇔ {w} = mk⇔ to from
      where
      to : InImage σ w → Σ[ p ∈ w ≤ʷ image-of-top ] LeastAbove.elt (β-above ⊤₁ p) [ σ ] ≈ʷ w
      to (a , e₁ , e₂) =
        p , ≤ʷ-trans (least-image σ (β-above ⊤₁ p) a e₂) e₁ , LeastAbove.above (β-above ⊤₁ p)
        where
        p : w ≤ʷ image-of-top
        p = Equivalence.to leastAbove⇔below-top (β-above a e₂)

      from : Σ[ p ∈ w ≤ʷ image-of-top ] LeastAbove.elt (β-above ⊤₁ p) [ σ ] ≈ʷ w → InImage σ w
      from (p , e) = LeastAbove.elt (β-above ⊤₁ p) , e
```

#### Onto exactly when every generator is an image

The image of `σ` is all of `FL(Y)` exactly when it contains every generator
(`onto⇔generators`{.AgdaFunction}): the image is closed under the
operations, so it contains every term once it contains the generators.  By
Corollary 1.6 a generator lies in the image only when it is one of the images
`σ i` (`generator-in-image`{.AgdaFunction} of [Lattices.Free.Generation][]),
so `σ` is onto exactly when every generator is one of the `σ i`
(`onto⇔generators-are-images`{.AgdaFunction}).  With finitely many generators
the condition is decidable, so `σ` fails to be onto exactly when some generator
is none of the images (`not-onto⇔missed-generator`{.AgdaFunction}); for a
self-embedding of `FL(n)` this says that it is proper exactly when it misses a
generator.

```agda
module _ {n : ℕ} {Y : Type υ} (σ : Sub (Fin n) Y) where
  -- The image is everything exactly when it contains every generator.
  onto⇔generators : (∀ w → InImage σ w) ⇔ (∀ y → InImage σ (ℊ y))
  onto⇔generators = mk⇔ (λ onto y → onto (ℊ y)) from
    where
    from : (∀ y → InImage σ (ℊ y)) → ∀ w → InImage σ w
    from gens (ℊ y) = gens y
    from gens (u ∧̇ v) =
      let (a , e) = from gens u ; (b , f) = from gens v in a ∧̇ b , ∧̇-cong e f
    from gens (u ∨̇ v) =
      let (a , e) = from gens u ; (b , f) = from gens v in a ∨̇ b , ∨̇-cong e f

  -- Corollary 1.6: the image is everything exactly when every generator is one of
  -- the images.
  onto⇔generators-are-images : (∀ w → InImage σ w) ⇔ (∀ y → Σ[ i ∈ Fin n ] ℊ y ≈ʷ σ i)
  onto⇔generators-are-images = mk⇔ to from
    where
    to : (∀ w → InImage σ w) → ∀ y → Σ[ i ∈ Fin n ] ℊ y ≈ʷ σ i
    to onto y = let (a , e) = onto (ℊ y) in generator-in-image σ a (≈ʷ-sym e)

    from : (∀ y → Σ[ i ∈ Fin n ] ℊ y ≈ʷ σ i) → ∀ w → InImage σ w
    from images =
      Equivalence.from onto⇔generators λ y → let (i , e) = images y in ℊ i , ≈ʷ-sym e

module _ {n m : ℕ} (σ : Sub (Fin n) (Fin m)) where
  open Decision (_≟_ {m}) using ( _≈ʷ?_ )

  -- The image is not everything exactly when some generator is none of the images.
  not-onto⇔missed-generator :
    (¬ (∀ w → InImage σ w)) ⇔ (Σ[ y ∈ Fin m ] ∀ i → ¬ (ℊ y ≈ʷ σ i))

  not-onto⇔missed-generator = mk⇔ to from
    where
    images : ∀ y → Dec (Σ[ i ∈ Fin n ] ℊ y ≈ʷ σ i)
    images y = any? λ i → ℊ y ≈ʷ? σ i

    to : ¬ (∀ w → InImage σ w) → Σ[ y ∈ Fin m ] ∀ i → ¬ (ℊ y ≈ʷ σ i)
    to not-onto =
      let (y , ¬image) =
            ¬∀⟶∃¬ m _ images λ all
              → not-onto (Equivalence.from (onto⇔generators-are-images σ) all)
      in  y , λ i e → ¬image (i , e)

    from : Σ[ y ∈ Fin m ] (∀ i → ¬ (ℊ y ≈ʷ σ i)) → ¬ (∀ w → InImage σ w)
    from (y , missed) onto =
      let (i , e) = Equivalence.to (onto⇔generators-are-images σ) onto y in missed i e
```

#### Examples

In `FL(3)` on `x`, `y`, `z`, the sublattice generated by `x ∨ y` and `z`
contains `(x ∨ y) ∧ z` and `z ∨ (x ∨ y)`, and contains neither `x` nor
`x ∨ z`, each decided by `inImage?`{.AgdaFunction} and read off by
`from-yes` or `from-no`.

```agda
private
  x y z : LatTerm (Fin 3)
  x = ℊ 0F
  y = ℊ 1F
  z = ℊ 2F

  -- The pair x ∨ y, z.
  τ : Sub (Fin 2) (Fin 3)
  τ 0F = x ∨̇ y
  τ 1F = z

  -- (x ∨ y) ∧ z and z ∨ (x ∨ y) lie in ⟨x ∨ y, z⟩.
  meet-in : InImage τ ((x ∨̇ y) ∧̇ z)
  meet-in = from-yes (inImage? _≟_ τ ((x ∨̇ y) ∧̇ z))

  join-in : InImage τ (z ∨̇ (x ∨̇ y))
  join-in = from-yes (inImage? _≟_ τ (z ∨̇ (x ∨̇ y)))

  -- x and x ∨ z do not.
  x-out : ¬ InImage τ x
  x-out = from-no (inImage? _≟_ τ x)

  x∨z-out : ¬ InImage τ (x ∨̇ z)
  x∨z-out = from-no (inImage? _≟_ τ (x ∨̇ z))
```
